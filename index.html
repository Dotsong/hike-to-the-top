A June & Pip adventure

Trail to the Top

There is a small mountain behind June and Pip’s house. From the treehouse it looks about as tall as a thumb. Today, with backpacks packed and sneakers double-knotted, the sisters are going to walk all the way up it.

Mom packed sandwiches. Dad drew a map. Pip packed nine snacks, “just in case.”

Sound on · drag to look around

Those are the Book Worms. They inch about on the leaves keeping an eye on things, and they are here to make sure everyone is having fun and learning something.
